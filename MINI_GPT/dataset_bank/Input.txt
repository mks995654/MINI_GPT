Customer Support SOP for Digital Payments

When a customer reports a failed digital transaction:

Verify customer identity using the bank's authentication process.
Obtain the transaction reference number (UTR or equivalent).
Check the transaction status in the banking system.
Determine whether the transaction is successful, pending, or failed.
Inform the customer of the expected resolution timeline.
If the transaction remains unresolved beyond the standard processing period, register a formal complaint or dispute.
Advise the customer not to initiate duplicate transactions unless necessary.
